5th May — Your Special Day
Dear Samra,

Today, on this golden day that the world marks as your birthday, I find myself at a loss for words — not because I have nothing to say, but because what I feel is far too vast to fit inside the small confines of language.

You are the kind of person who changes rooms simply by walking into them. There is warmth in your presence that no winter can touch, and a light in your eyes that no darkness can dim.

"You make ordinary moments feel like core memories. Thank you for being exactly who you are — irreplaceable, extraordinary, deeply loved."

On this birthday, I wish for you not just happiness, but the deep and steady kind — the sort that lives in your chest on quiet mornings and doesn't wait for special occasions. I wish you courage for every dream, warmth for every cold day, and laughter that finds you easily.

May this year be the one where everything you've quietly hoped for begins to fall into place. May people love you well, may opportunities find you, may joy be not a destination but your constant companion.

Samra, the world is honestly a better place because you are in it. Never forget that.

from,
Maroof Lone 🌹
